How ParkLanes Works

A frictionless loop connecting driver booking to physical lane validation in seconds.

Step 01

Scan QR at Entry

The driver scans the dynamic operator QR code at the parking facility entrance. The browser instantly prompts registration and entry verification.

Step 02

Operator Validation

The parking operator's app immediately detects the check-in request, verifies availability, and approves the vehicle entry with one tap.

Step 03

Park Seamlessly

The driver parks at their designated spot. Real-time city occupancy counters update instantly, keeping metrics live for other users.
